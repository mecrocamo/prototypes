Hero Motion Comparison

Hero carousel: crossfade vs. slide

CTV home, 1920×1080. Both options auto-advance every 9 seconds and stay on the same slide, so you can switch views at any point.

Slide 1 of 6

Crossfade

  • Outgoing text fades out over 200ms.
  • Background crossfades over 600ms from 100ms, settling from 1.05 to 1.0 scale.
  • Incoming text fades in and rises 24px from 300ms, 300ms each, staggered 60ms.
  • Content changes in place, with no sense of direction.

Slide

  • Outgoing and incoming art move together one screen width over 850ms, right to left on Next.
  • Outgoing text fades out over 180ms. Incoming text fades in from a 120px offset from 240ms, 600ms each, staggered 40ms.
  • Prev reverses the direction, so the motion follows the D-pad press.
